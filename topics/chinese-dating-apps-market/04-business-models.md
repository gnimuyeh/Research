# Business Models & Monetization in Chinese Dating Apps

## Overview

Chinese dating apps have developed monetization strategies strikingly different from Western subscription-based models. Virtual gifting, live streaming, and tiered VIP systems generate the bulk of revenue, though regulatory pressure is forcing diversification.

## 1. Virtual Gifting + Live Streaming (The Dominant Model)

### How It Works

1. Users purchase in-app currency with real money (e.g., "Momo Coins")
2. Users buy virtual gifts ranging from 0.1 RMB to 1,888.8 RMB (~$290)
3. Gifts are sent to livestreamers during broadcasts (visible to all viewers)
4. Revenue split ~50/50 between platform and streamer
5. Top streamers earn tens of thousands of dollars monthly

### Scale

- At peak, Momo's live streaming generated **$194.8 million in a single quarter** (~80% of total revenue)
- In Q2 2021, Douyu alone received **2B+ RMB ($341M)** from virtual gifts
- Momo's most-purchased items are under $5, but the two most popular items are virtual currency purchases (40%+ of total orders)

### Cultural Roots

Virtual gifting builds on China's **hongbao (红包)** tradition — red envelopes given at holidays and celebrations. WeChat digitized this in 2014, normalizing digital money transfers as social gestures. The model has been described as "as lucrative as gaming."

### Regulatory Impact

Since 2020, a series of crackdowns has constrained live streaming gifting:
- Teen-mode restrictions prevent under-18 users from gifting
- Limits on daily gifting amounts for some platforms
- Impact on Hello Group: live streaming revenue dropped from **80% to 48%** of total revenue
- FY2025 net income fell 22% YoY to RMB 804M

## 2. Freemium / VIP Subscriptions

### Tantan VIP

- **Pricing**: ~$10-20/month (vs. Tinder's ~$30/month in China)
- **Features**: See who liked you, unlimited swipes, profile boosts, "Super Like" equivalents
- **Paying users**: Declined from 1.2M → 0.6M over two years

### Momo VIP

- Tiered membership with increasing privileges
- Features include profile visibility boosts, advanced filters, messaging priority
- **Paying users**: Declined from 7.4M → 3.9M (FY2023-FY2025)

### Soul Coins

- Micropayment system for specific features
- Purchase voice matching sessions, extended conversation time
- Avatar customization items
- RMB 2.21B total revenue (2024) with 83%+ gross margins

### Parent App Subscriptions

- Perfect In-Laws: **299-1,299 yuan** per tier
- "Super exposure" premium features to boost child's profile visibility
- Family-building Matchmaking: Similar tiered pricing

## 3. Offline-to-Online Hybrid (Matchmaking Services)

### Baihe Jiayuan / Lovelink

- **200+ physical matchmaking centers** across China
- Premium one-on-one consultant services
- Online profiles feed into offline matchmaking sessions
- High-touch, high-price model targeting serious marriage seekers

### Zhenai

- **40+ physical stores**
- Personal matchmaking consultants
- Premium pricing for personalized, private service
- Targets high-income professionals

## 4. Host-Guided Video Matchmaking

### Yi Dui's Model

- Professional "red lady" hosts moderate video blind dates
- Revenue from virtual gifts sent to hosts and participants
- The most profitable dating app: **RMB 262 million net profit (H1 2025)**
- Popular in lower-tier cities where other apps have less penetration

## 5. Xiaohongshu's Emerging Model

- "Unicorn" gifts cost **299 potato coins (~43 yuan / $5.95)**
- Split 50-50 with livestream matchmaking hosts
- Lower price points than Momo/Tantan gifting
- Revenue "depends on viewer mood" — smaller but growing

## 6. AI Companion Monetization (Emerging)

- Soul's EchoVerse: Customizable AI characters, likely freemium with premium personas
- Xingye/Glow: **Gacha mechanics** borrowed from gaming — users "pull" for AI boyfriend cards
- Xiaoice: Subscription for AI partner interactions
- **Market projection**: RMB 3.9B (2025) → RMB 59.5B (2028)

## Revenue Comparison

| Company | FY2025 Revenue | Primary Model |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Hello Group (Momo+Tantan) | RMB 10.37B (~$1.4B) | Live streaming + VIP + virtual gifts |
| Soul | RMB 2.21B (~$300M) | Soul Coins + VIP + ads |
| Yi Dui | ~RMB 524M+ (extrapolated) | Host-guided matchmaking + virtual gifts |
| Baihe Jiayuan | Not publicly disclosed | Offline services + online subscriptions |

## The Revenue Model Crisis

The industry faces a fundamental challenge: **no model has matched the profitability of peak virtual gifting.**

Current diversification attempts:
- **International expansion**: Hello Group targeting RMB 3B overseas revenue by 2026 (overseas up 71% YoY)
- **AI features as paid add-ons**: Chat assistance, greeting generation, AI companions
- **Subscription restructuring**: Higher ARPU from fewer, more committed paying users
- **Advertising**: Dating-adjacent services and brand partnerships
- **Platform integration**: Services within WeChat Mini Programs
