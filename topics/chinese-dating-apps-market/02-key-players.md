# Key Players in the Chinese Dating App Market

## Tier 1: Market Leaders

### Hello Group (NASDAQ: MOMO) — Momo + Tantan

**Parent company**: Hello Group Inc. (formerly Momo Inc.)
**Headquarters**: Beijing
**FY2025 Revenue**: RMB 10.37 billion (~$1.4B)
**Net Income**: RMB 804 million (down 22% YoY)

**Momo** (陌陌):
- Launched 2011 as a location-based social app
- **MAU**: 66.7 million
- **Paying users**: Declined from 7.4M → 5.7M → 3.9M over two years
- Pivoted from dating to live streaming in 2015; live streaming was 80% of revenue at peak, now 48%
- Positioned as social discovery + entertainment, not purely dating

**Tantan** (探探):
- Launched 2014, acquired by Momo in 2018 for ~$800M
- **MAU**: 50M+ (estimated)
- **Paying users**: Declined from 1.2M → 0.6M over two years
- China's closest Tinder equivalent (swipe-based matching)
- Uses Nash equilibrium-inspired matching to optimize system-wide outcomes
- Now split into domestic and international versions

**Strategy shift**: Hello Group pivoting hard to international markets — overseas revenue up 71% to RMB 2.0B in FY2025, targeting RMB 3B by 2026.

### Soul (NASDAQ pending / HK IPO pending) — Soulgate Inc.

**Parent company**: Soulgate Inc.
**Major shareholder**: Tencent (49.9%)
**Revenue**: RMB 2.21 billion (2024)
**Gross margins**: 83%+
**Registered users**: 390 million
**DAU**: 11 million
**MAU**: ~30 million

- Launched 2016 by Zhang Lu (张璐)
- **Avatar-based, personality-first** — no photos, no real names
- Users matched via personality quiz ("Soul Mode") into five types
- First messages must be audio, not text
- Near-balanced gender ratio: 52.4% male, 47.6% female
- 60% of users aged 18-24 (Gen Z stronghold)
- Average daily engagement: 40 minutes
- Developed proprietary LLM "Soul X" for emotion recognition
- EchoVerse AI companion feature launched 2024
- CVPR 2025 paper accepted; won MER24 challenge at ACM MM 2024
- Pending Hong Kong IPO

## Tier 2: Traditional Matchmaking Platforms

### Baihe Jiayuan / Lovelink (百合佳缘)

**Parent company**: Baihe Jiayuan Group (owned by Fosun International)
**MAU**: 7.9 million (2022)
**Physical stores**: 200+

- Formed from 2015 merger of Baihe (百合网, founded 2005) and Jiayuan (世纪佳缘, founded 2003)
- Jiayuan was the first Chinese dating site to IPO on NASDAQ (2011)
- **Marriage-oriented**: Profiles resemble resumes — salary, education, car/property ownership, credit score, all verified with documentation
- Rebranded to "Lovelink" (合缘) in recent years
- Hybrid online-to-offline model with physical matchmaking centers
- Targets serious marriage seekers, typically 25-40 age range

### Zhenai (珍爱网)

**Physical stores**: 40+
**Model**: Premium offline matchmaking with online component

- Founded 2005
- One-on-one matchmaking consultants
- Premium pricing for personalized service
- Targets higher-income professionals seeking marriage
- Emphasis on privacy and discretion

## Tier 3: Innovators and Niche Players

### Yi Dui (伊对)

**Net profit**: RMB 262 million (H1 2025) — most profitable dating app
**Model**: Host-guided video matchmaking

- Unique model: Professional "red lady" hosts moderate video blind dates between matched users
- Users don't interact 1-on-1 initially; hosts facilitate conversation
- Revenue from virtual gifts to hosts and participants
- Particularly popular in lower-tier cities

### QingChiFan (请吃饭)

**Model**: Meal-based dating
- Users invite matches to dinner at a specific restaurant
- Inviter sets criteria (age, profession, zodiac)
- Bill-splitting arranged in advance
- Restaurant choice signals personality and income
- Higher actual-date conversion rates than swipe apps

### Perfect In-Laws (亲家) and Parent-Facing Apps

**Perfect In-Laws users**: 50M+
**Family-building Matchmaking**: 2M+ users, claims 53K marriages since 2020

- Parents create profiles for their children (sometimes without consent)
- Display salary, car/property ownership, state vs. private sector employment
- Red Thread Matchmaking (WeChat Mini Program): Phone interview verification, 20 daily profile recommendations
- Subscription pricing: 299-1,299 yuan per tier

### Blued (蓝城兄弟)

- China's largest LGBTQ+ dating app
- **Removed from Chinese app stores in November 2025** amid tightening LGBTQ+ regulation
- Previously had 49M+ registered users
- Parent company BluedCity Holdings went public on NASDAQ in 2020

## Tier 4: Platform Dating (Non-Dedicated Apps)

### Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RedNote)

**Platform MAU**: 300M+
- Not a dating app, but emerged as a major dating venue through "Nine Box" blind date livestreams (early 2024)
- 8 participants in video grid, hosted by "cyber matchmakers"
- ~110,000 views nightly; Lunar New Year 2025 saw 66 streams averaging 15M+ viewers across 8 provinces
- Audience connects via DMs

### Douyin (抖音 / TikTok China)

**Platform MAU**: 828M
- Hosts matchmaking livestreams
- Major advertising platform for dating apps
- Increasingly absorbing dating functionality organically

## The AI Companion Sector (Adjacent Market)

**Projected growth**: RMB 3.9 billion (2025) → RMB 59.5 billion (2028)

Key players:
- **Xingye/Glow** (MiniMax): Gacha-style AI boyfriend cards; dominated by female users
- **Xiaoice** (小冰): 17 million virtual partners; Microsoft spinoff
- **Soul EchoVerse**: Customizable AI characters within the dating platform
- **Maoxiang / Duxiang**: Other AI companion apps

**Critical difference from West**: US market skews toward AI girlfriends for young men; Chinese market skews toward AI boyfriends for adult women rejecting traditional patriarchal relationship dynamics.
