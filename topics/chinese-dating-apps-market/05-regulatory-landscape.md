# Regulatory Landscape: Chinese Dating App Regulation

## Overview

Chinese dating apps operate under one of the world's most comprehensive and evolving regulatory frameworks. Real-name registration, content moderation requirements, AI regulations, and live streaming rules all shape what platforms can build and how they monetize.

## 1. Real-Name Registration

**Requirement**: All dating app users must verify their identity with a real name linked to their national ID.

- Enforced through the **Cybersecurity Law of the PRC** (effective June 2017)
- All major platforms connect to China's national identity verification system
- **National Cyber ID system** (in beta as of 2024): 70+ apps testing a unified system combining facial recognition + national ID verification + phone number linking
- Users cannot create anonymous accounts (though apps like Soul allow pseudonymous presentation after verification)

## 2. Facial Recognition Regulations

**Facial Recognition Technology Application Security Management Regulations** (effective June 2025):

- Facial recognition **cannot be the sole verification method**
- Users **cannot be coerced** into facial recognition
- Encrypted storage required for all biometric data
- Regular security audits mandated
- Creates tension with dating apps that rely on facial recognition for profile verification (e.g., Tantan)

## 3. Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)

**China's GDPR equivalent** (effective November 2021):

- Explicit consent required for collecting sensitive personal data
- Dating app data (sexual orientation, relationship preferences, location) classified as sensitive
- Cross-border data transfer restrictions affect international operations
- Right to deletion and data portability
- Penalties: Up to 5% of annual revenue or 50 million yuan

## 4. Live Streaming Regulations

Multiple overlapping regulations constrain live streaming on dating platforms:

- **Teen-mode restrictions**: Under-18 users cannot send virtual gifts
- **Tipping limits**: Some platforms face daily gifting caps
- **Content moderation**: Real-time monitoring requirements for live streams
- **Host certification**: Professional livestreamers must register and follow content guidelines
- **Tax enforcement**: Crackdown on streamers evading income taxes (high-profile cases with fines of 600M+ yuan)

These regulations have directly caused Hello Group's live streaming revenue decline from 80% to 48% of total revenue.

## 5. AI Companion Regulations

The government views AI companions as potentially threatening to marriage and birth rates:

- **Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services** (effective August 2023): AI-generated content must align with "socialist core values"
- Specific targeting of "addiction and dependence on anthropomorphized interaction"
- AI companions cannot express views contrary to state ideology
- Content filtering required for AI-generated messages
- In practice: apps like Glow/Xingye have faced periodic content reviews

## 6. LGBTQ+ Content Restrictions

- **Blued** (China's largest LGBTQ+ dating app) was **removed from Chinese app stores in November 2025**
- LGBTQ+ content increasingly restricted across all platforms
- Dating apps cannot promote or normalize same-sex relationships in their marketing
- Some apps have removed same-sex matching options from their Chinese versions

## 7. Content Moderation Requirements

- All user-generated content subject to real-time moderation
- Platforms must employ human + AI moderation teams
- Pornographic content, fraud, and "vulgar" content result in platform penalties
- Dating apps face particular scrutiny for facilitating prostitution or escort services
- Momo historically struggled with this reputation; invested heavily in moderation

## 8. Anti-Fraud Measures

- Platforms required to implement fraud detection and reporting systems
- "Romance fraud" (杀猪盘, "pig butchering") is a national concern
- Cross-platform coordination with law enforcement
- Apps must display fraud warnings and educational content
- Tantan claims to block millions of fraudulent accounts annually

## 9. Demographic Policy Intersection

The government's concern over declining birth rates creates a unique regulatory dynamic:

- Some local governments have partnered with dating apps for state-sponsored matchmaking events
- Potential future interventions discussed in policy circles:
  - Mandating marriage-oriented features
  - Restricting AI companion apps
  - Subsidizing dating app subscriptions for certain demographics
  - Requiring match-to-marriage conversion reporting
- No country has the combination of demographic urgency, state capacity, and platform scale to attempt this
