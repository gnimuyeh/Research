# Key Sources, Reports, and Technical Work

## Academic & Research Papers

### 1. "Seeking Soulmate via Voice: Understanding Promises and Challenges of Online Synchronized Voice-Based Mobile Dating"
- **Published**: arXiv, 2024 (arXiv:2402.19328)
- **Relevance**: Examines the design and user experience of voice-based dating platforms in China, particularly Soul's voice matching paradigm where users remain anonymous and connect through audio rather than photos.

### 2. "Teller: Real-Time Streaming Audio-Driven Portrait Animation with Autoregressive Motion Generation"
- **Venue**: CVPR 2025
- **Authors**: Soul App research team
- **Relevance**: Soul's AI research on real-time portrait animation -- the technology underlying their avatar-based video chat system where facial expressions are projected onto animated avatars.

### 3. Soul App at ACM MM 2024 (Multimodal Emotion Recognition Challenge - MER24)
- **Venue**: ACM International Conference on Multimedia, 2024
- **Relevance**: Soul achieved top position at the Multimodal Emotion Recognition Challenge, demonstrating their capabilities in understanding human emotions across text, voice, and visual modalities -- core to their "emotion-first" AI approach.

### 4. "Virtual Gifting on China's Live Streaming Platforms: Hijacking the Online Gift Economy"
- **Authors**: Published in Chinese Journal of Communication, Vol 12, No 3
- **Year**: 2019
- **Publisher**: Taylor & Francis (Tandfonline)
- **Relevance**: Foundational analysis of how virtual gifting monetization works on Chinese platforms, the revenue-sharing model between platforms and streamers, and the cultural roots of digital gift-giving in Chinese society.
- **URL**: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17544750.2019.1583260

### 5. "Understanding Virtual Gifting in Live Streaming by the Theory of Planned Behavior"
- **Authors**: Xu et al.
- **Venue**: Human Behavior and Emerging Technologies (Wiley), 2022
- **Relevance**: Theoretical framework for understanding why users spend real money on virtual gifts, using behavioral psychology to explain the mechanics of the gifting economy.
- **URL**: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2022/8148077

### 6. "Seeking Love and Companionship through Streaming: Unpacking Livestreamer-moderated Senior Matchmaking in China"
- **Venue**: CHI 2023 (ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems)
- **Relevance**: Qualitative study of 6 livestreaming matchmakers and 12 senior match-seekers (ages 50-70) on Chinese platforms, examining how live streaming enables matchmaking for older adults.
- **URL**: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3544548.3581195

### 7. "Transformation of China's Most Popular Dating App, Momo, and Its Impact on Young Adult Sexuality"
- **Published**: 2022 (ResearchGate)
- **Relevance**: Critical social construction of technology analysis examining how Momo's design features and business model transformations have shaped sexual behavior and relationship norms among Chinese young adults.

### 8. "The Politics of Dating Apps: Gender, Sexuality, and Emergent Publics in Urban China"
- **Publisher**: MIT Press (Open Access)
- **Relevance**: Book-length study of how dating apps intersect with gender politics, sexuality, and public discourse in urban China.
- **URL**: https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph/5048/The-Politics-of-Dating-AppsGender-Sexuality-and

## Industry Reports & Key Articles

### 9. TechNode - "Tantan's Matching Strategy: You Can Be Matched, But Possibly Not With Your Soul Mate" (2018)
- **URL**: https://technode.com/2018/07/05/tantans-matching-strategy-you-can-be-matched-but-possibly-not-with-your-soul-mate/
- **Relevance**: Rare inside look at Tantan CEO Wang Yu's philosophy on matching algorithms, including the application of Nash equilibrium theory to dating optimization.

### 10. Soul App - "Redefines Human-AI Interaction with Full-Duplex Technology at WAIC 2025"
- **URL**: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/soul-app-redefines-human-ai-interaction-with-full-duplex-technology-at-waic-2025-302517041.html
- **Relevance**: Technical announcement of Soul's end-to-end full-duplex voice model that eliminates traditional VAD (Voice Activity Detection) mechanisms, enabling AI to autonomously determine conversation rhythms.

### 11. ChinaTalk - "Why America Builds AI Girlfriends and China Makes AI Boyfriends"
- **URL**: https://www.chinatalk.media/p/why-america-builds-ai-girlfriends

### 12. Sixth Tone - "On China's New Dating Apps, Parents Make the Match" (2023)
