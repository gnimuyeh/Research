# Learning Path: Chinese Dating App Technology & Innovation

## Stage 1: Understand the Cultural and Market Context

### Why Start Here
You cannot understand Chinese dating app design without understanding the cultural forces that shape it. Every technical decision -- from avatar systems to salary verification -- is a response to specific cultural pressures.

### Key Topics
1. Chinese dating culture vs. Western dating culture
2. The role of family/parents in matchmaking
3. The "leftover women" (剩女) phenomenon and marriage pressure
4. China's gender imbalance (33 million more men than women)
5. 996 work culture and urban isolation (240 million singles)
6. The Great Firewall and independent tech ecosystem

## Stage 2: Map the Major Platforms

### Key Topics
1. **Momo/Hello Group**: From dating app to live streaming social platform (2011-present)
2. **Tantan**: China's Tinder clone that evolved beyond Tinder (acquired by Momo 2018 for ~$800M)
3. **Soul**: The anti-Tinder -- avatar-based, personality-first, Gen Z focused (2016-present)
4. **Baihe Jiayuan / Lovelink**: Marriage-oriented, resume-style profiles, asset verification
5. **QingChiFan**: Meal-based dating without Western equivalent
6. **Parent-facing apps**: Perfect In-Laws, Family-building Matchmaking, Red Thread Matchmaking
7. **Platform dating**: Xiaohongshu livestream blind dates, Douyin matchmaking streams

## Stage 6: Analyze Safety, Trust, and Regulation

### Key Topics
1. **Real-name registration system**: National ID verification requirements
2. **National Cyber ID system**: 70+ apps testing (2024), facial recognition + ID + phone linking
3. **China's 2025 Facial Recognition Technology regulations**: Opt-out requirements, data protection mandates
4. **Fraud landscape**: ~20% of encounters involve fraud; "yuepao" extortion schemes; photo manipulation culture
5. **AI-generated deepfakes**: Increasing use of synthetic faces/videos to defeat verification
6. **Spoofing market**: Taobao/Xianyu vendors selling facial recognition bypass services
7. **AI companion regulation**: Government monitoring emotional dependency, restricting content against "socialist ideology"
