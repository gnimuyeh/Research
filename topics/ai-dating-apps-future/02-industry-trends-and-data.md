# Dating App Industry Trends & Data (2024-2026)

## 1. Dating Fatigue, Burnout, and Declining Satisfaction

### The Scale of the Crisis

- **79%** of dating app users report emotional fatigue, frustration, or burnout (Forbes Health, 2025)
- **78%** have experienced dating app burnout (defined as emotional, mental, or physical exhaustion)
- **80%** of women and **74%** of men report some level of burnout
- **More than 50%** of Gen Z feels burned out "often or always" — the highest of any age group
- **Only 12%** are completely satisfied with online dating experiences
- **39%** of people have used a dating app, but only **7%** are currently using one

### What's Driving Burnout

1. **Inability to connect**: 40% cite this as the biggest factor in exhaustion
2. **Disappointment and rejection**: The second most common cause
3. **Choice overload**: Too many options lead to decision paralysis (Schwartz's paradox of choice)
4. **Gamification**: Slot-machine-like interfaces create addictive but shallow use
5. **Ghosting**: 62% of active dating app users have been ghosted; 30% of all US adults
6. **Breadcrumbing**: Leading someone on with little effort, linked to loneliness and lower life satisfaction

### User Exodus

- **1.4 million** people left dating apps in the UK between 2023-2024
- Tinder alone lost **594,000** UK users in that period
- Bumble lost **368,000** UK users; Hinge lost **131,000**
- **51%** of American men had zero dates in 2025 (Hily State of Dating)

## 2. How Gen Z Dates Differently

### The Generational Shift

Gen Z is rethinking dating from the ground up:

- **75%+** feel burnt out on dating apps
- **33%** of Gen Z have never used a dating app
- Almost **50%** prefer meeting partners through mutual friends
- About **33%** hope to meet someone in day-to-day routine
- **65%** of 18-29 year olds have used dating apps, but usage is declining

### What Gen Z Values

1. **Authenticity over curation**: Filtered photos and curated profiles are losing favor; candid, unfiltered content preferred
2. **Niche interests**: 46% say quirky interests are key to attraction; 49% say "geeking out together" is intimate
3. **Higher standards**: 64% of women refuse to settle for less than they want
4. **Romance is back**: 52% of women are self-proclaimed romantics
5. **Group/social dating**: 42% say friends influence dating life; 37% plan group or double dates
6. **AI openness**: 60% of younger Gen Z (18-22) are open to AI dating help

### Apps Gen Z Prefers

- **Hinge**: 50%+ of users are Gen Z; seen as more relationship-oriented
- **Feeld**: Record 841,000 downloads in Q1 2025; aligns with openness about gender and sexuality
- **Thursday**: Opens only Thursday evenings; fights burnout through time-limiting
- **Tinder**: Trying to win Gen Z back with Double Date (85% of users aged 18-29) and College Mode
- **Bumble**: Struggling; lost 90% of stock value since 2021 IPO

### The IRL Movement

Gen Z is driving a return to in-person connection:
- "Champagne and Shackles" matchmaking parties organized by Gen Z professionals
- Speed dating events gaining popularity
- Members clubs (Lox Club, Ambyr Club, We Met IRL)
- Dating apps pivoting to host live events (Hinge's $1M social group fund; Bumble IRL)

## 3. The "Dating App Apocalypse" Discourse

### Financial Evidence

| Company | Metric | Detail |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Match Group | Market cap decline | -82% from 2021 peak ($46.58B to $7.53B) |
| Match Group | Paying users (Q4 2025) | 13.8M, down 5% YoY |
| Tinder | Subscribers | -8% YoY (Q4 2025) |
| Tinder | Revenue (Q1 2025) | -7% YoY |
| Bumble | Market cap decline | -92% since 2021 IPO |
| Bumble | Paying users (Q3 2025) | 3.6M, down 16% YoY |
| Bumble | Revenue (Q3 2025) | -10% YoY |
| Bumble | Layoffs (June 2025) | 30% of workforce |

### The Counter-Argument: Not Dead, Reckoning

- The market is still valued at $11.6 billion and growing
- Hinge grew downloads 25.4% YoY and revenue 23% (Q1 2025)
- Grindr grew revenue 28% in full-year 2025 with nearly 15M MAUs
- Feeld hit record downloads in Q1 2025
- New entrants keep entering the market
- The problem is with the dominant model, not the concept of digital dating itself

### The Verdict

The industry is not dying — it is being forced to evolve. The swipe-based, gamified model that built the industry is losing its appeal. Companies that pivot toward intentionality, authenticity, and AI-enhanced (not AI-replaced) connection will survive. Those that cling to engagement-maximizing mechanics will continue to lose users and revenue.

## 4. Emerging Approaches

### Slow Dating
- Apps limiting matches (daily limits, compatibility drops)
- **Thursday**: Opens only Thursday 6PM-midnight; fights burnout
- China's slow dating apps limit messages and emphasize voice over photos
- Enforced pauses between replies to encourage reflection
- Bio unlocks only after mutual interest

### Video-First Dating
- By 2024, 40% of users had tried video-based features
- Video chats show 20% higher engagement than text
- 50% of daters prioritize safety, making video "vibe checks" popular
- Apps embedding video prompts directly into profiles
- OkCupid virtual game nights, video date features

### Voice-Based Dating
- **Known**: SF startup using voice AI for onboarding; 80% of introductions led to IRL dates; raised $9.7M
- More than 50% of users now opt for voice intros or video responses
- Daily voice-based challenges ("Tell your best joke")
- Photos blurred until users exchange voice interactions
- In-app voice calls with conversation starters

### AI Wingman Concepts
- **WingAI**: Claims 2.5x reply rate; users screenshot chats for AI-generated replies
- **Rizz**: ~1.5M monthly active users
- **YourMove**: Over 1M downloads
- **Tinder**: Launched "The Game Game" AI flirting practice
- **Grindr**: AI wingman targeting 2027 launch
- **Bumble**: Founder envisions AI concierges that "date" other concierges
- 26% of US singles use AI to enhance dating (333% increase YoY)
- Concerns: dependency, authenticity erosion, inequality amplification

### VR/Metaverse Dating
- **Flirtual** and **Nevermet**: First dates as avatars in digital settings
- Appeals to gamers, introverts, and long-distance couples
- Early-stage but growing interest
